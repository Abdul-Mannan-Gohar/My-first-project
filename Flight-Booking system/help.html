Help Center

Find answers to the most common questions. Still need help? We’re here 24/7.

1. Booking a Flight

How do I search and book a flight?

Go to the homepage → Fill in departure, destination, dates & passengers → Click “Search Flights” → Choose your flight → Enter passenger details → Complete secure payment.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes! Just enter their full name exactly as it appears on their passport/ID.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Affirm (availability depends on your country).

I didn’t receive my confirmation email

Check your spam/junk folder first. Still missing? Go to “Manage Booking” with your reference number and last name to view or resend it.

2. Managing My Booking

How do I retrieve my booking?

Click “Manage Booking” → Enter your 6-character booking reference (e.g., ABC123) and the passenger’s last name.

Can I change or correct a name?

Minor spelling corrections are free within 24 hours. Full name changes are usually not permitted for security reasons.

How do I select or change my seat?

In “Manage Booking” → Seat Selection (small fee may apply depending on fare).

3. Payment Issues

Why was my payment declined?

Common reasons: incorrect CVV/expiry, insufficient funds, or your bank flagged the transaction. Try another card or contact your bank.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes — Klarna, Affirm, and PayPal Credit are available at checkout in supported countries.

4. Check-in & Boarding

When does online check-in open?

48 hours before international flights, 24 hours before domestic flights.

Do I need to print my boarding pass?

No — mobile boarding passes are accepted at almost all airports.

5. Baggage Allowance

What baggage is included?

Economy: 1 carry-on (7 kg) + 1 personal item
Premium/Business: 2 carry-ons + 1 checked bag (23 kg)

How to add extra baggage?

Cheapest online via “Manage Booking” → Extra Services. More expensive at the airport.

6. Flight Changes & Cancellations

Can I change my flight date/time?

Flexible fares → free changes
Standard fares → change fee + fare difference
Changes allowed up to 3 hours before departure.

What is the cancellation policy?

• Within 24h of booking (flight 7+ days away) → full refund
• Non-refundable tickets → only taxes refunded
• Flexible tickets → full or partial refund

My flight was cancelled by the airline

You are entitled to a full refund OR free rebooking + meals/hotel if needed. EU/UK flights may qualify for up to €600 compensation (EU261).

7. Special Assistance

How do I request wheelchair assistance?

Request at least 48 hours before departure in “Manage Booking” → Special Assistance.

Can I travel with a service dog?

Yes, free of charge in the cabin. Upload required documents 48 hours in advance.
